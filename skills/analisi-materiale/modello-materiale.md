# Template of materiale.md

Write `<work>/.help-me-write/materiale.md` with exactly these sections, in this order,
in the user's language. Remove the angle-bracket guidance.

```markdown
# Materiale

## Sintesi del lavoro
<five to ten lines: what the work is, its goal, what was done, with what; only facts
found in the material or told by the user>

## Mappa del materiale
| Percorso | Cosa contiene | Per quale parte del documento |
|---|---|---|
| <path relative to the work folder> | <content in one line> | <chapter or section it feeds> |

## Cronologia
| Data | Evento | Fonte |
|---|---|---|
| <date or period> | <what happened> | <file, git, diary, or "utente"> |

## Dati e numeri
| Valore | Significato | File e posizione |
|---|---|---|
| <value with unit> | <what it measures> | <file and position, or "risposta dell'utente, <date>"> |

## Note dell'autore
<constraints from README, notes and diaries, each with its file>

## Non letto
<files and folders not read, and why>

## Lacune e domande
<what the document will need and the material does not give, as questions>
```
